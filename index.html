    01 / About

    About me

    I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science at the University of Nottingham, a founding member of the Cyber Physical Health and Assistive Robotics Technologies (CHART) research group, and an affiliated researcher with the NIHR Nottingham Biomedical Research Centre (Precision Imaging), the Institute of Mental Health and the Centre for Dementia. I am a Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and a member of the MICCAI Society, IEEE and BMVA.

    My research focuses on trustworthy, explainable and uncertainty-aware AI for healthcare. I develop label-efficient (semi- and self-supervised) and multimodal deep learning methods for medical imaging, neuroimaging and radiomics, with a particular interest in the early detection of dementia, alongside embodied AI and assistive robotics that help people living with frailty stay well at home. Working closely with clinicians, I aim for AI that is accurate, honest about what it does not know, and practical in real clinical settings.

    I bring over 20 years of academic and industrial experience. Before joining Nottingham in 2020 I was Principal Lecturer (equivalent to Associate Professor) and Programme Leader at De Montfort University, and earlier worked as a senior software architect in industry. My work has appeared at venues including MICCAI (Best Paper Award runner-up, 2026) and ICCV, I serve as Track Chair for ACM TRUST 2027, and I have supervised six PhD researchers.

    I am always happy to hear from prospective students, clinical partners and industry. See Join Me for available projects, or Consultancy for ways to work together.
